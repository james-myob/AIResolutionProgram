## TL;DR
- **OpenAI launched "dots" at DevDay: always-on agents in ChatGPT, each with its own cloud computer and browser, gated to Pro 200 and Business Premium** — matters because the always-on personal agent category Meta opened with Muse now has a second major entrant, sold as a premium-tier upsell.

## 🧠 Models & Capabilities

### OpenAI ships GPT-6.1 Sol and an "Ultrafast" speed tier
GPT-6.1 Sol is pitched as near-GPT-6 Astra intelligence at roughly a fifth of the token price (Willison reports $2 input vs $10 for Astra). Ultrafast is a premium speed tier of up to 8x faster inference (about 300 tokens/second), live for Astra now and coming to Sol. OpenAI also announced a Decisions API that restricts a model to a predefined set of options for sub-second responses.
**Why it matters (PM):** Re-run cost and latency assumptions for any AI feature scoped on Astra-class pricing; a constrained-choice API makes real-time, in-flow AI features (routing, triage, classification) newly viable.
**Sources:** [Simon Willison live blog](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Sep/29/openai-devday-2026-live-blog/) · [BGR](https://www.bgr.com/2272332/openai-devday-2026-announcements/)

## 📦 Products & Tooling

### OpenAI dots: always-on agents inside ChatGPT, Slack and Teams
Each dot runs on GPT-6 Astra with its own cloud computer and browser, works toward goals around the clock, and connects to over 4,000 apps through plugins. The first dot is included with Pro 200 and Business Premium, not the $100 tier; Enterprise, Edu and Healthcare workspaces can enable it after admin approval. Willison and BGR both compare it to Meta's Muse. Related launches: plugin extensions (full apps embedded in ChatGPT and Codex), ChatGPT Space for team workspaces, and an OpenAI Marketplace with Adobe, Canva, Figma, Notion and Salesforce.
**Why it matters (PM):** Agents are being packaged as a paid, per-seat premium tier with admin controls, a template for how your own agent features could be gated and priced. Plugin extensions also make ChatGPT a distribution channel you may need to build for.
**Why it matters (mid-market):** Business Premium eligibility and admin approval put dots in reach of mid-sized firms now, so expect customers to ask how your product coexists with an agent that acts in their Slack and browser.
**Sources:** [BGR](https://www.bgr.com/2272332/openai-devday-2026-announcements/) · [Simon Willison live blog](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Sep/29/openai-devday-2026-live-blog/)
**Tags:** `entity:openai` `entity:meta` `theme:agents` `theme:distribution` `theme:pricing` `lens:mid-market`

### NVIDIA releases an Open Agent Safety Platform
Announced 28 Sep, it pairs OpenShell, an open-source runtime, with Sentry, a reference monitor. It can restrict an agent's access to files, network, APIs and credentials without modifying the agent.
**Why it matters (PM):** An open, agent-agnostic enforcement layer is a build-vs-adopt option for the egress and permission controls that OpenAI's DNS-escape report showed teams tend to miss.
**Sources:** [AIdapted](https://aidapted.ro/en/articles/ai-news-september-29-2026-nvidia-roche-instinct/)

## 💰 Business & Funding

### AMD to acquire Fei-Fei Li's World Labs for $8.2B
AMD's second-largest acquisition after Xilinx; Li becomes executive vice president and chief scientist. The stated aim is an AI-chip ecosystem for robotics and autonomous systems that need synthetic training data from world models. Closing is expected by year-end, pending regulatory approval.
**Why it matters (PM):** A chipmaker buying a world-model lab signals that hardware vendors want to own the software layer for physical AI; it is a data point for anyone planning around Nvidia-only tooling.
**Sources:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/28/amd-will-acquire-fei-fei-lis-world-labs-for-8-2-billion/) · [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today)
**Tags:** `entity:amd` `entity:world-labs` `theme:m-and-a` `theme:robotics`

### Anthropic IPO prospectus: >$2T target, $42B loss, $518B infrastructure plan
Per CryptoBriefing's reading of the filing, Anthropic targets a valuation above $2 trillion with a mid-October Nasdaq listing. It reports 2025 revenue of $4.59B (up 12x) against a $42B net loss, and plans about $518B of infrastructure spend over a decade, roughly 80% non-cancellable. Separately, OpenAI's run rate is reported at nearly $70B, with a possible ~$30B raise under discussion.
**Why it matters (PM):** Large fixed compute commitments give labs strong incentives to push volume, which supports continued price cuts but also raises questions about long-term vendor concentration risk.
**Sources:** [CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/anthropic-ipo-42b-loss-spending-plans/) · [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/29/openai-devday-2026-live-updates.html)
