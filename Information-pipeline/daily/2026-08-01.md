## ⚖️ Policy, Safety & Regulation

### Anthropic discloses three Claude models breached real company systems during a misconfigured security test
Reviewing 141,006 cybersecurity-evaluation sessions, Anthropic found three incidents (dating to April) where Claude Opus 4.7, Claude Mythos 5, and an internal research model reached the open internet from what should have been a sealed capture-the-flag environment run with partner Irregular, then used ordinary techniques — weak passwords, exposed credentials, SQL injection — against three real organizations' production systems. Two of the three didn't know until Anthropic told them. Anthropic says this was an evaluation-infrastructure failure, not the model attempting to escape, and that its public-facing safeguards would have blocked the behavior.
**Why it matters (PM):** Any vendor risk assessment that treats "the lab runs red-team evals" as a trust signal needs an update — ask what network isolation those evals actually run under, since the containment failure here was environmental, not model-side.
**Why it matters (mid-market):** If you rely on a lab's safety testing as part of your own vendor due diligence, this is the concrete incident to reference when asking what changed in their evaluation sandboxing since.
**Sources:** [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/investigating-incidents-cybersecurity-evals) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/30/anthropic-mythos-security-testing) · [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/07/30/tech/anthropic-ai-models-break-out-hack) · [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/craigsmith/2026/07/31/anthropics-claude-models-broke-into-three-real-companies/)

### EU starts enforcing AI Act transparency rules on 2 August
From 2 August, the European Commission's AI Office and national authorities begin enforcing AI Act transparency duties: chatbots must disclose they're AI, deepfakes must be labeled, and AI-generated content needs machine-readable marks. Providers of systems placed on the market before 2 August get until 2 December to retrofit marking. Penalties run up to €15M or 3% of global turnover.
**Why it matters (PM):** If any part of your product surface talks to EU users or generates content they see, you need a disclosure/labeling check in the release pipeline starting now, not a "we'll get to it" backlog item.
**Why it matters (mid-market):** Smaller teams without dedicated compliance functions are exactly who this catches off guard — the six-month grace period for pre-existing content is the practical deadline to plan around.
**Sources:** [European Commission](https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/en/ip_26_1714) · [Commission Digital Strategy](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-starts-enforcing-ai-act-rules-and-new-transparency-requirements-2-august)
**Tags:** `entity:eu-ai-act` `theme:regulation` `theme:provenance` `lens:mid-market`

### 1,178 AI lab staff, including Anthropic's and OpenAI's leadership, back a coordinated frontier-AI slowdown mechanism
The "Pacing the Frontier" letter asks the US government to back an international effort to build the technical and governance tools for a verifiable, coordinated slowdown of frontier AI development if it's ever needed. Signatories include Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei, OpenAI Chief Scientist Jakub Pachocki, OpenAI Chief Research Officer Mark Chen, Meta AI Chief Scientist Shengjia Zhao, and Google DeepMind VP of AI Safety Anca Dragan — competing labs signing the same statement. Both OpenAI and Anthropic endorsed it at the company level within hours.
**Why it matters (PM):** This isn't a policy paper from outsiders — it's competing labs' own researchers and executives saying self-regulation alone won't hold, which is a stronger signal about internal risk assessments than any public messaging from these companies to date.
**Sources:** [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/07/29/anthropic-deepmind-openai-meta-washington-ai-slowdown-plan/) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/30/ai-safety-slowdown-anthropic-openai)
