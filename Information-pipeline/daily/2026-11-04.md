### CrowdStrike launches Falcon Guardian, a kill switch for rogue AI agents
CrowdStrike's Falcon Guardian lets administrators stop dangerous AI agents, prevent them relaunching and restrict their network access (CNBC, via The Neuron).

## 💰 Business & Funding

### Revolut reportedly cuts AI coding costs ~90% by defaulting to self-hosted GLM
The Register reports Revolut serves GLM-5.3 on 56 Nvidia B200 GPUs and claims a 60% improvement in coding time and 70% in lead time across roughly 2,000 engineers. Cheap tokens reportedly encouraged wider use for debugging and Jira tickets; vision queries still route to Gemini or GPT since GLM is text-only. Figures are company-reported.
**Why it matters (PM):** Model choice for internal dev tooling is a cost lever, not just a quality one; a routed setup (cheap self-hosted default, frontier model for hard or multimodal tasks) is worth modelling for your own spend.
**Why it matters (mid-market):** Self-hosting needs GPU capacity and ops skill that most mid-sized firms lack, so the transferable lesson is routing, not necessarily hosting.
**Sources:** [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/11/03/british-fintech-revolut-slashes-ai-coding-costs-10x-by-cutting-out-claude-and-going-self-hosted/5307345) · [The Neuron](https://www.theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-wednesday-november-4-2026/)

## 🔬 Research & Papers

### Anthropic releases FlashProve: zero-knowledge proofs that a reply came from a specific model
The experimental open-source system has a provider publish a commitment to its weights, then generate a succinct proof per batch of requests that a user can check on a CPU. On Llama 4 Maverick (400B MoE) it proves about 110 tokens/s on one H100 at roughly 19 MB per 1,024-token request. Limits: it covers only an integer version of the model under greedy decoding, caps context at 1,024 tokens, doesn't hide architecture, and the fast verifier lacks formal guarantees.
**Why it matters (PM):** Not shippable, but it points to future "verified model" claims in contracts and compliance, relevant if customers ever need proof of which model handled their data.
**Sources:** [Anthropic Research](https://www.anthropic.com/research/provable-inference) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/03/anthropic-is-working-on-a-system-to-prove-ai-models-identity/)
