## 🧠 Models & Capabilities

### Gemini 4 Carbon gets independent results: strong value, mixed behaviour
Gemini 4 Carbon is positioned for long-horizon agentic work, with Gemini 4 Argon still Google's recommended default. Vals ranks it #3 at $10.51 per test versus $17.94 for Fable 5.2, and ARC Prize reports 94.2% on ARC-AGI-2 at $0.38 per task. Testers also reported hallucinations and verbosity, and Roboflow ranked it last of 10 on vision speed and cost. These figures are relayed from posts on X.
**Why it matters (PM):** Add Carbon to your model bake-off before its price doubles on 1 December ($2/$10 to $4/$20 per million tokens in Cursor), and test for hallucination on your own tasks.
**Sources:** [Latent Space](https://latent.space/p/ainews-mercor-750m-series-d-at-20b-valuation-3b-run-rate/) · [Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/10/llm-gemini/)

## 📦 Products & Tooling

### Google's Gemini Decisions API: a cheap model just for judgments
Gemini Decision 1 answers yes/no, multiple-choice and rubric-scoring questions over text, images, audio, video and PDFs. It costs 10 cents per million input tokens with free output, the same as OpenAI's GPT-6 Luna; Simon Willison notes Jev is cheaper at 4.2 cents.
**Why it matters (PM):** Classification-style features (triage, moderation, grading) can be built on a dedicated judgment endpoint rather than a general chat model, so re-check your per-call cost assumptions.
**Why it matters (mid-market):** Near-free output makes high-volume document and inbox checks affordable inside mid-market per-seat pricing.
**Sources:** [Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/11/gemini-decisions-api/)
**Tags:** `entity:google` `theme:ai-cost` `lens:mid-market` `tier:t5`

### AWS releases an official local emulator for its services
aws-emulator lets teams run tests against local clones of many AWS services without a paid API key. It is written in Rust, largely by a swarm of AI agents, and currently needs Docker on macOS and Windows.
**Why it matters (PM):** Faster, free integration testing shortens the loop for AI-assisted development on AWS-backed products.
**Sources:** [Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/11/aws-emulator/)
**Tags:** `entity:aws` `theme:agents` `tier:t5`

## 💰 Business & Funding

### Anthropic: roadshow video and S-1 figures
Anthropic's roadshow says it trains and serves Claude across four chip families (Trainium, TPUs, NVIDIA and AMD GPUs) and defines "total effective compute" as physical capacity times the "compute multipliers" it discovers; a 4x multiplier means a model needing 100 units trains on 25. Latent Space, citing posts on X, reports the filing shows an $81B run rate at 31 October and $17B Q3 revenue, implying $1.24–1.38T fully diluted at $70–78 per share (unverified at source).
**Why it matters (PM):** Multi-chip supply and a public run rate reduce single-vendor risk for Claude, but treat the figures as unconfirmed until you read the prospectus.
**Sources:** [Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/11/anthropic-ipo-roadshow/) · [Latent Space](https://latent.space/p/ainews-mercor-750m-series-d-at-20b-valuation-3b-run-rate/) · [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/roadshow-launch)
**Tags:** `entity:anthropic` `theme:ipo` `tier:t1` `tier:t5` `tier:t2`

### Mercor raises $750M Series D at $20B
Mercor, which supplies expert human data to AI labs, doubled its valuation from $10B (October 2025). It reports $3B annualised revenue, up from $1B in February and $2B in June, 5 million experts, and says it spends 3x more on inference than salaries (company claims).
**Why it matters (PM):** Paid expert evaluation and training data is a growth market, which signals that domain-specific quality, not raw model access, is where AI product differentiation is being bought.
**Sources:** [Latent Space](https://latent.space/p/ainews-mercor-750m-series-d-at-20b-valuation-3b-run-rate/)

## 🛠️ Product Practice

### OpenAI on working with Codex: cloud environments and voice
At the AI Engineer Code Summit, OpenAI's guidance was to set up a cloud environment once with the needed GitHub repos and run multiple isolated tasks in it, handing local work off when needed. It also suggested voice for iterative edits and lowering effort ("turn it down").
**Why it matters (PM):** Parallel isolated agent tasks are becoming the default workflow, so plan review and approval capacity for the PRs they produce.
**Sources:** [Latent Space](https://latent.space/p/codex-work/)
