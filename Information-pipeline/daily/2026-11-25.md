## 🔬 Research & Papers

### OpenClaw ShellBench: the harness matters almost as much as the model
ShellBench ran 12 models across five agent harnesses (100 tasks, 53,100 graded runs). GPT-6 Astra scored 65.3% in Pi but 55.4% in Claude Code, while Claude Fable 5.2 scored 61.0% and 63.0% in the same two. Per The Neuron, the best-to-worst model gap is 45.7 points versus 6.2 points from the harness alone.
**Why it matters (PM):** Benchmarks that fix one harness can mislead; test candidate models inside the harness you will actually ship.
**Sources:** [The Neuron](https://www.theneuron.ai/news/openclaw-shellbench-ai-agent-harnesses/) · [The Deep View](https://www.thedeepview.com/articles/exclusive-openclaw-offers-an-agent-reality-check/)

## ⚖️ Policy, Safety & Regulation

### Amazon: open-source agent autonomously exploited a zero-day
Amazon Threat Intelligence says an agent built on the open-source pentest tool Cairn, told only to "obtain shell access", exploited an unknown flaw in an upload endpoint of an open-source medical imaging app and got file-write access in about 11 minutes, then root on an East Asian university's server in September. Cairn has also been adopted by hacking groups and linked to attacks on 100+ sites.
**Why it matters (PM):** Time from vulnerability to exploit is shrinking, so prioritise upload-handling hardening and faster patch cycles.
**Why it matters (mid-market):** Smaller customers running self-hosted open-source components are most exposed; consider offering managed patching.
**Sources:** [Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/amazon-says-open-source-ai-agent-mostly-autonomously-exploited-a-zero-day-2000844891) · [The Neuron](https://www.theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-november-25-2026/)
**Tags:** `entity:amazon` `entity:cairn` `theme:security` `theme:autonomous-offense` `lens:mid-market` `tier:aggregator`

### UK FCA sets expectations for agentic AI in financial services
The FCA says tech providers and social platforms must decide whether their agentic products carry out regulated activities (payments, promotions, advice) and seek authorisation if so. Regulated firms remain responsible when they build, deploy or rely on agentic AI, must understand each system's autonomy, and keep senior managers accountable under Consumer Duty. A planned Agentic Policy Sprint is mentioned.
**Why it matters (PM):** Agents that act on money or advice carry regulatory scope; build autonomy limits and audit trails in from the start.
**Why it matters (mid-market):** Accounting and finance software vendors serving regulated clients should expect customers to ask for evidence of agent controls.
**Sources:** [FCA](https://www.fca.org.uk/firms/innovation/ai-approach/agentic-ai)
**Tags:** `entity:fca` `theme:agentic-regulation` `theme:financial-services` `lens:mid-market` `tier:lab-official`
