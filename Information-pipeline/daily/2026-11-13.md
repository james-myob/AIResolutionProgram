## 🧠 Models & Capabilities

### Google ships Gemini 4 Hydrogen, its lowest-cost Gemini 4 model
Google priced Hydrogen at $1.50 input / $7.50 output per million tokens, flat across a 1M-token context window, and says it finishes tasks about 2.5x faster than Gemini 3.8 Flash. It scores 51 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at default thinking (54 at xhigh) versus 50 for Gemini 4 Argon; Latent Space reports independent figures of about 200 tokens/s and $3.16 per task at xhigh, and one tester saw runaway reasoning costs. It is the new free-tier model and powers Google Search AI features.
**Why it matters (PM):** Re-baseline cost-per-task for any agent or coding feature, using measured per-task cost rather than list price, since Hydrogen is only ~25% cheaper than Carbon/Argon until their 1 Dec doubling.
**Why it matters (mid-market):** A free-tier, Search-integrated model raises customer expectations for "free" AI features, squeezing what mid-market products can charge extra for.
**Sources:** [Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-hydrogen/) · [Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/12/llm-gemini/) · [Latent Space](https://latent.space/p/ainews-jensen-huang-resigns-after-33-years-leading-nvidia/)

### Thinking Machines releases Inkling 2 Small as Apache 2.0 open weights
A 594B-total / 17B-active mixture-of-experts (a design that activates only part of the model per token, making it cheaper to run) with text and image input and up to 1M context. Latent Space relays that it is the #1 US open model on Code Arena WebDev at 1647, with day-0 vLLM and SGLang support. Figures come from the lab's X post.
**Why it matters (PM):** A permissively licensed open model near the top of a coding leaderboard strengthens the self-host option when you negotiate or model vendor costs.
**Sources:** [Latent Space](https://latent.space/p/ainews-jensen-huang-resigns-after-33-years-leading-nvidia/)

## 📈 Industry Analysis

### Jensen Huang resigns as NVIDIA CEO
NVIDIA said Huang resigned as president, CEO and director effective immediately. CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO, Harvey C. Jones is first board chair, and the board is searching internally and externally. NVIDIA expects operations and roadmap to continue uninterrupted and reports Q3 FY27 earnings on 17 Nov. **Why it matters (PM):** The 17 Nov call is the first test of supply and roadmap continuity, which matters if your AI feature costs assume current GPU pricing.

## ⚖️ Policy, Safety & Regulation

### Claim: Chinese frontier models were trained on Blackwell chips; unverified
Will Depue says weight-initialisation patterns reveal the GPU type, and that six Chinese models (including DeepSeek-V4-Pro, Kimi K3, MiniMax-M3) were initialised on Blackwell systems, around 2–3K GPUs for the largest. These are unverified claims from X; critics say the scale is small versus China's compute and may show export controls partly working. A reported Commerce rule on Chinese access to overseas data centres would reportedly affect at most 14% of 2027 Chinese compute (one analyst's estimate).
**Why it matters (PM):** Treat this as a signal to watch export-control exposure of any Chinese open-weight model in your stack, not as an established fact.
**Sources:** [Latent Space](https://latent.space/p/ainews-jensen-huang-resigns-after-33-years-leading-nvidia/) (relaying [Depue](https://x.com/willdepue/status/2120992457630326841))
