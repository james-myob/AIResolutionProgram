## 🧠 Models & Capabilities

### Mistral releases Leanstral 1.5, a free open model for formal verification
Mistral shipped Leanstral 1.5, an Apache-2.0 model (119B total / 6B active parameters) specialised for formal proof-writing in Lean 4. It hits 100% on miniF2F, solves 587 of 672 PutnamBench problems at roughly $4/problem, and autonomously found five previously unknown bugs — including a Rust integer-overflow vulnerability — across 57 tested repositories.
**Why it matters (PM):** Formal verification has been a research niche because it was too expensive to run broadly; a free model that finds real bugs at ~$4/problem makes it a plausible addition to a CI pipeline, not just an academic exercise.
**Why it matters (mid-market):** Free and open-weight means smaller engineering teams can add automated bug-hunting to their pipeline without a security-research budget.
**Sources:** [Mistral AI](https://mistral.ai/news/leanstral-1-5/)

## 📦 Products & Tooling

### Anthropic overhauls Claude API rate limits and adds admin spend controls
Anthropic replaced its spend-based Claude API rate-limit tiers with a usage-based system (Start, Build, Scale) that gives developers roughly 5x more headroom on Sonnet and Haiku, with higher limits requestable via the Claude Console. Separately, it shipped new Claude Enterprise admin tools: usage/cost breakdowns by team, a natural-language "Analytics Chat" for querying spend, model-default entitlements to restrict expensive models by role, and spend-threshold alerts at 75%/90% of org limits.
**Why it matters (PM):** If your team has been rate-limited or has been flying blind on who's burning budget on which model, both constraints just loosened — worth revisiting any workaround you built around the old limits.
**Why it matters (mid-market):** Smaller teams without a dedicated FinOps function get enterprise-grade cost visibility for free; the model-default entitlements make it realistic to let more employees use Claude without an open-ended bill.
**Sources:** [Anthropic](https://claude.com/blog/giving-admins-more-visibility-and-control-over-claude-usage-and-spend) · [AlphaSignal](https://alphasignal.ai/news/anthropic-gives-claude-api-builders-5x-more-room-to-scale)

## 🔬 Research & Papers

### "Program-as-Weights" compiles fuzzy tasks into small, reusable models instead of repeated LLM calls
Researchers from Waterloo, Cornell, and Harvard propose compiling natural-language specifications for "fuzzy" classification-style tasks into compact neural artifacts rather than calling an LLM API on every request. A 4B-parameter compiler produces parameter-efficient adapters for a small frozen interpreter; a 600M-parameter interpreter running these compiled programs matches a 32B model's performance on a laptop-class chip.
**Why it matters (PM):** If a feature just does the same kind of fuzzy judgment call repeatedly (tagging, routing, moderation-style decisions), this is a preview of a pattern that could cut per-request inference cost by an order of magnitude — worth flagging to your eng team if a high-volume feature is currently just wrapping an LLM call.
**Why it matters (mid-market):** Running near-frontier-quality judgment calls on consumer-grade hardware removes the API-cost ceiling that's kept some always-on AI features out of reach for smaller companies.
**Sources:** [Hugging Face Daily Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.02512)
**Tags:** `entity:hugging-face` `theme:efficiency` `theme:research` `lens:mid-market`

## ⚖️ Policy, Safety & Regulation

### Anthropic details Fable 5 cybersecurity safeguards and a joint "Cyber Jailbreak Severity" scale
Following last week's reversal of Fable 5's export restrictions, Anthropic published details of the model's new safeguards: a four-tier classifier (prohibited, high-risk dual-use, low-risk dual-use, benign) designed to deliberately avoid blanket-blocking cybersecurity tasks so defensive security work stays possible. Alongside it, Anthropic introduced the Cyber Jailbreak Severity (CJS) scale — CJS-0 (informational) through CJS-4 (critical) — developed jointly with Amazon, Microsoft, and Google to standardise how jailbreak severity gets scored across the industry.
**Why it matters (PM):** A shared severity taxonomy across four major labs is the closest thing yet to an industry safety standard; if you sell into regulated or security-conscious customers, expect "what's your CJS exposure" to show up in vendor-security questionnaires within the next few quarters.
**Sources:** [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/fable-safeguards-jailbreak-framework) · [The Neuron Daily](https://theneuron.ai/p/july-2-thursday) · [The Rundown AI](https://www.therundown.ai/p/anthropic-fable-returns-worldwide)

## 📒 New Concepts

### Cyber Jailbreak Severity (CJS) scale
**Plain English:** A standardised 5-point scale (CJS-0 "informational" through CJS-4 "critical") for rating how dangerous a successful jailbreak of an AI model's cybersecurity safeguards actually is — the same idea as CVSS scoring for software vulnerabilities, but applied to AI jailbreaks. It was developed jointly by Anthropic, Amazon, Microsoft, and Google, meaning it's not one lab's internal metric but a shared cross-industry attempt at a common yardstick.
**Origin:** [Anthropic — Fable 5 cyber safeguards](https://www.anthropic.com/news/fable-safeguards-jailbreak-framework)
**Tag:** `concept:cyber-jailbreak-severity-scale`
