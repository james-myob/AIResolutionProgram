## 📦 Products & Tooling

### OpenAI moves ChatGPT and Codex to one shared usage allowance from December
Per The Neuron, citing OpenAI's help documentation, eligible ChatGPT and Codex usage will draw from a single allowance starting in December. Ordinary Instant chats won't count against it. Plus, Pro and Business users will see remaining allowance and reset times in Settings, with extra credits available after the limit. (Secondhand; OpenAI's own page was not fetched.)
**Why it matters (PM):** A shared pool turns "how much coding agent can I use" into a visible product constraint, so expect customers to ask for the same transparency (remaining balance, reset time, top-up credits) in your own AI features.
**Sources:** [OpenAI help docs](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001625-understanding-chatgpt-and-codex-usage-limits) · [The Neuron](https://www.theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-monday-november-2-2026/)
**Tags:** `entity:openai` `entity:chatgpt` `entity:codex` `theme:pricing` `tier:t5`

### Parameter Labs: chain of flaws let a crafted ChatGPT share link read other users' private data
Parameter Labs, via Simon Willison, describes a chain through ChatGPT's sandboxed HTML previews: a MathLive keyboard handler that accepted `postMessage` calls and wrote HTML into the parent page, combined with a content-security-policy that allowed a YouTube oEmbed endpoint to reflect JavaScript. A signed-in user opening a crafted share URL could have had private data fetched and exfiltrated. OpenAI closed the hole and paid a $5,000 bounty, per the write-up.
**Why it matters (PM):** If your product renders model-generated HTML or apps inside iframes, the lesson is that a sandbox plus a permissive CSP and third-party libraries is not isolation; review that surface before shipping interactive outputs.
**Sources:** [Parameter Labs](https://www.parameter.ai/labs/we-found-a-gap-at-openai) · [Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Oct/31/how-we-took-over-chatgpt-accounts/)

## 💰 Business & Funding

### OpenAI reportedly lowers new-round target to $20B from $30B
TechCrunch, summarising the Financial Times and Wall Street Journal, reports the target fell from $30B to $20B, with UAE investors leading and about $11B secured. The WSJ says some large investors were "tepid". Earlier reports put the valuation at $1.25T versus a $1.4T goal. OpenAI has not confirmed these terms.
**Why it matters (PM):** The market is repricing even the leading lab ahead of IPO season, so treat single-vendor roadmap bets as carrying more financing risk than they did a quarter ago.
**Sources:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/31/openai-reportedly-lowers-funding-target-for-new-round/) · [Latent Space](https://www.latent.space/p/ainews-google-admits-ai-mode-hurts-search-revenue-15-500b-drop-worst-since-2004-ipo-ai-bear-market-c)

### Salesforce agrees to buy Rune Technologies, a military supply-planning software maker
Salesforce announced a definitive agreement to acquire Rune. Later reports put the price at about $375M; Salesforce did not disclose terms. The Next Web frames it as an expansion of Salesforce's defence-focused offering.
**Why it matters (PM):** Another incumbent buying a vertical workflow vendor, rather than building, shows where platform vendors think agents need domain data and process depth.
**Why it matters (mid-market):** Vertical acquisitions by the big CRM players raise the bar for mid-market vendors competing on industry-specific AI; differentiation needs to come from workflow depth, not a generic agent layer.
**Sources:** [Salesforce](https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/salesforce-signs-definitive-agreement-to-acquire-rune-technologies/) · [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/salesforce-rune-technologies-acquisition-missionforce-defence)

## ⚖️ Policy, Safety & Regulation

### Anthropic's usage-policy update adds a ban on sustained abuse of Claude; effective 12 November
The 8 Oct update, effective 12 Nov, also adds rules for Claude controlling hardware that takes autonomous physical actions (human observation, stop capability and a safe state if disconnected), explicitly bars weapons software and arming drones, and rewrites the surveillance and law-enforcement sections. The abuse rule targets sustained, needless cruelty only; ordinary frustration, dark creative themes and testing are excluded. It drew coverage this weekend after CNN reported permanent bans are possible in extreme cases.
**Why it matters (PM):** The hardware-control and surveillance clauses apply to anything you build on Claude, so check your roadmap for agents that act on physical systems or profile people before 12 November.
**Sources:** [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/2026-usage-policy-update) · [CNN](https://edition.cnn.com/2026/11/01/business/anthropic-dont-be-mean-claude-ban)
**Tags:** `entity:anthropic` `entity:claude` `theme:usage-policy` `tier:t1`

### Census survey switches from "AI" to "Super Intelligence" wording after executive order
Simon Willison reruns a 2025 corporate-adoption chart from the Census Bureau's BTOS survey. After a Nov 2025 wording change, large-firm adoption reads about 40.7% (Oct 2026); the 19 Oct 2026 wording change, following Executive Order 14434, now asks about "Super Intelligence or SI". Published numbers still use the old AI wording; the first SI-worded results were due the following Thursday. Willison notes the two wordings are not comparable.
**Why it matters (PM):** Government adoption statistics are about to break series, so don't cite year-over-year Census AI-adoption trends in market sizing without checking which question wording they use.
**Why it matters (mid-market):** The same release shows adoption by size band (e.g. 50-99 employees 27.9%, 100-249 33.5%, versus 40.7% for 250+), a usable benchmark for mid-market penetration.
**Sources:** [Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/2/census-bureau-super-intelligence/)
**Tags:** `entity:us-census-bureau` `theme:ai-adoption` `lens:mid-market` `tier:t5`

## 📈 Industry Analysis

### Google says AI Mode monetises less; Alphabet falls ~15% while Meta ad revenue grows 26%
Latent Space reports Google's Philipp Schindler cited "the lower monetization we were seeing in AI Mode", alongside a roughly 15% (~$500B) share drop it calls the worst since the 2004 IPO (headline claim, not independently verified). CNBC, via The Neuron, reports Meta's Q3 advertising revenue rose 26% against 12% for Google Search & other, and AWS grew 39.3% to nearly $46B, its fastest in almost five years.
**Why it matters (PM):** AI answers are shifting value from search ads toward AI-targeted ads and infrastructure, so any plan that depends on organic or paid search traffic needs a downside case.
**Sources:** [Latent Space](https://www.latent.space/p/ainews-google-admits-ai-mode-hurts-search-revenue-15-500b-drop-worst-since-2004-ipo-ai-bear-market-c) · [CNBC (Meta vs Google)](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/01/ai-meta-ads-google-search.html) · [CNBC (AWS)](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/29/aws-q3-earnings-report-2026.html)
