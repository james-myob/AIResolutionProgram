## 🧠 Models & Capabilities

### Microsoft ships MAI-Thinking-1 — first in-house reasoning model, trained without OpenAI

At Build 2026 (June 2–3, San Francisco), Microsoft launched MAI-Thinking-1: a 35B-active-parameter sparse MoE reasoning model with a 256K context window, trained from scratch on commercially licensed data with no third-party model distillation. It scores 97.0% on AIME 2025, matches Claude Opus 4.6 on SWE-Bench Pro, and was preferred over Claude Sonnet 4.6 in blind human evaluations. Alongside it, MAI-Code-1-Flash (5B params) began rolling out inside GitHub Copilot across all tiers, outperforming Claude Haiku 4.5 by 16 points on SWE-Bench Pro coding tasks.

**Why it matters (PM):** Product teams building on GitHub Copilot or Azure AI Foundry now have a Microsoft-native reasoning model whose roadmap and pricing are not tied to OpenAI — reducing the model-dependency risk that has quietly sat under most Microsoft AI bets.

**Why it matters (mid-market):** Paired with Microsoft's new "Autopilots" (always-on agents with Entra ID governance for every action), this is the clearest mid-market AI bundle pitch yet: one vendor, one contract, one governed-agent framework for the full stack.

## ⚖️ Policy, Safety & Regulation

### Anthropic MITRE report: AI-enabled cyberattack risk up 1.7× in one year

Anthropic's red team published analysis of 832 accounts banned for malicious cyber use between March 2025 and March 2026, mapped to MITRE ATT&CK. Medium-or-high-risk threat actors using AI for cyber operations jumped from 33% to 56% of the pool — a 1.7× increase. The nature of attacks is shifting: phishing assistance fell 8.6% while lateral movement and autonomous "agentic" kill-chain orchestration — where a model makes real-time pivot decisions without human intervention — rose sharply. Anthropic flags that MITRE ATT&CK has no entries for autonomous AI orchestration and is in active discussion with MITRE to extend the framework.

**Why it matters (PM):** If your product grants any AI agent persistent, privileged access to internal systems — code repos, CRM, communication tools — this threat model describes what's already being attempted against similar targets. Brief your security team with the framework-gap finding: a standard MITRE-based security review will miss autonomous agentic attacks.

**Sources:** [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/AI-enabled-cyber-threats-mitre-attack) · [resultsense.com](https://www.resultsense.com/news/2026-06-04-anthropic-ai-cyber-threats-mitre-attack/) · [News9live](https://www.news9live.com/technology/tech-news/ai-cyber-threat-anthropic-report-hackers-beyond-phishing-2977043)

### EU AI Act omnibus: Annex III deadlines deferred 16 months, two new prohibitions confirmed

On May 7, the EU Council, Parliament, and Commission provisionally agreed to amend the AI Act before its first compliance wave. Annex III high-risk AI obligations (recruitment, education, credit scoring, law enforcement systems) deferred from August 2026 to December 2027 — a 16-month reprieve. Annex I product-safety AI (medical devices, lifts, radio equipment) deferred one year to August 2028. Two new prohibitions take effect December 2026: AI generating non-consensual intimate images and CSAM. Formal adoption and publication expected June–July 2026.

**Why it matters (PM):** The 16-month Annex III deferral shifts the HR-tech, fintech, and legal-AI compliance sprint into a structured programme. If your roadmap included "AI Act compliance by August" as a deadline, the target has moved — but December 2027 is an 18-month window, not an indefinite reprieve.

**Why it matters (mid-market):** Mid-market companies serving EU customers with AI-assisted hiring, lending, or service-prioritisation tools had a very short runway to August. The deferral buys structured compliance time — use it to build, not to wait.

**Sources:** [EU Council](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/05/07/artificial-intelligence-council-and-parliament-agree-to-simplify-and-streamline-rules/) · [Gibson Dunn](https://www.gibsondunn.com/eu-ai-act-omnibus-agreement-postponed-high-risk-deadlines-and-other-key-changes/) · [IAPP](https://iapp.org/news/a/ai-act-omnibus-what-just-happened-and-what-comes-next) · [Inside Privacy](https://www.insideprivacy.com/artificial-intelligence/eu-ai-act-update-timeline-relief-targeted-simplification-and-new-prohibitions/)
