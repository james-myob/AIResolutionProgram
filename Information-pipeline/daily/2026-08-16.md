## 🧠 Models & Capabilities

### Google ships Gemini 3.7 Flash, undercutting its own three-week-old model on price
Google released Gemini 3.7 Flash, pitched as its workhorse model for coding and agentic workflows, just three weeks after Gemini 3.6 Flash. It's priced at an introductory $0.75 per million input tokens / $3.75 per million output tokens through the end of 2026 — half of what 3.6 Flash cost at launch — before doubling to $1.50/$7.50 on 1 January 2027.

**Why it matters (PM):** The low-cost tier is now iterating faster than most product roadmaps can track; if you've built a cost model or a "good enough" model tier into a feature plan, revisit it before assuming it's still the cheapest viable option.
**Why it matters (mid-market):** A temporary 50% price cut on a capable coding/agent model lowers the near-term cost floor for smaller teams shipping AI features now — but the 1 January price doubling means budgets planned around today's rate need a hard expiry date attached.
**Sources:** [Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-3-7-flash/) · [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/googles-gemini-3-7-flash-targets-coding-and-agents-with-a-50-introductory-price-cut) · [Tech Times](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/324366/20260813/google-launches-gemini-37-flash-coding-gains-half-price-through-year-end.htm)

## 📦 Products & Tooling

### OpenAI previews Ultrafast mode: GPT-5.6 Sol at up to 14x the speed, powered by Cerebras
OpenAI began a limited preview of Ultrafast, a new API tier that runs GPT-5.6 Sol at up to 750 output tokens per second — up to 14x faster than the standard tier — using Cerebras hardware, with the same underlying model intelligence. Access is currently restricted to a select group of customers testing latency-sensitive workloads like incident response, voice support, and live coding assistance.

**Why it matters (PM):** This is the first mainstream signal that inference speed, not just capability or price, is becoming a distinct product lever labs will sell separately — worth tracking as a design constraint for any feature where response latency currently limits what you can build (voice, live coding, real-time agents).
**Sources:** [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/previewing-ultrafast/) · [Cerebras](https://www.cerebras.ai/blog/accelerating-gpt-5-6-sol-ultrafast-with-openai) · [HPCwire](https://www.hpcwire.com/off-the-wire/cerebras-powers-openais-gpt-5-6-sol-ultrafast-mode/)

## 💰 Business & Funding

### Google DeepMind resets its leadership: Hassabis steps up, Kavukcuoglu steps in, Jeff Dean steps out
Alphabet named Demis Hassabis Chief Scientist of Alphabet and Chair of Google DeepMind, handing day-to-day operations to Koray Kavukcuoglu, who becomes DeepMind's SVP/CEO reporting directly to Sundar Pichai. Hassabis says the move lets him focus on long-horizon AGI strategy and Isomorphic Labs. The reshuffle follows the departure of Jeff Dean (leaving to start a company with Sanjay Ghemawat) and comes after reporting that described internal morale problems, a talent exodus, and slipped model deadlines inside DeepMind.

**Why it matters (PM):** A leadership change at the company shipping Gemini is a signal worth watching for roadmap volatility over the next two quarters — new leadership under pressure to show results often means faster ship cycles but also more reshuffled priorities; don't build long-lead-time integrations on the assumption that Gemini's current cadence or positioning holds steady.
**Sources:** [Google](https://blog.google/company-news/inside-google/message-ceo/next-chapter-ai-momentum/) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/06/demis-hassabis-google-reshuffle-deepmind-role.html) · [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/08/10/how-stalled-models-missed-deadlines-and-staff-burnout-lead-to-the-unraveling-of-googles-deepmind/)
**Tags:** `entity:google-deepmind` `entity:google` `theme:org-change`

### Anthropic signs $9.1B Riot Platforms compute deal, weighs its largest-ever acquisition in $6B Decart talks
Anthropic struck a 20-year, $9.1B compute deal with bitcoin miner Riot Platforms, leasing 191 megawatts at Riot's Texas campus (potentially $16.1B if extended). Separately, Anthropic is reportedly in talks to acquire Israeli AI infrastructure startup Decart — which builds software that makes chips run training and inference more efficiently — for about $6B, roughly 50% above its last funding valuation. Neither deal is Anthropic's first infrastructure move this year; it follows a $10B deal with cloud startup Volta Infra and a $45B compute commitment from xAI/SpaceX.

**Why it matters (PM):** Anthropic buying an efficiency-tooling company rather than only leasing more raw compute suggests it's treating chip/training efficiency as a competitive lever in its own right, not just a cost line — a pattern worth watching if you're evaluating vendors whose margins depend on inference cost trends staying flat.
**Sources:** [Bloomberg — Riot](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-11/anthropic-strikes-9-billion-deal-with-cloud-computing-firm-riot) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/11/riot-platforms-signs-anthropic-deal-as-miners-shift-to-ai-infrastructure-.html) · [Bloomberg — Decart](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-13/anthropic-said-in-talks-to-buy-ai-startup-decart-for-6-billion) · [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/08/13/anthropic-said-in-talks-to-buy-startup-decart-for-6-billion/)

## 📈 Industry Analysis

### a16z's Alex Danco: "100% AI-generated" is becoming shorthand for "not worth reading" — and that's a problem
Alex Danco argues that "100% of this text is AI" has become a reflexive way to dismiss writing, regardless of quality, and that this reaction pattern resembles preference falsification — people performing skepticism of AI content because it's socially safe, not because they've actually evaluated it.

**Why it matters (PM):** If your product surfaces AI-generated or AI-assisted content to users (summaries, drafts, support replies), expect a growing share of users to discount it on sight rather than on merit — labeling and framing choices in the UI may matter more for adoption than further quality improvements.
**Sources:** [a16z](https://a16z.com/this-essay-is-10-ai-generated/)
