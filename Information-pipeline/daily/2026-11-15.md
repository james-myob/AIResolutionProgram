# Daily AI Briefing — Sun 15 Nov 2026

## TL;DR
- **Hackers reached some Notion customers' AI chat transcripts through AI provider Fireworks** — matters because your AI subprocessors are part of your breach surface and your customers' trust.

## 💰 Business & Funding

### Anthropic shares proposed IPO price range
Anthropic posted an expected price range of $70 to $78 per share for its proposed offering. The offering had not yet become effective at the time of reporting. This follows the 10 Nov roadshow launch.
**Why it matters (PM):** Plan for vendor disclosures (risk factors, financials) becoming public, which will feed procurement questions from enterprise customers.
**Sources:** [Anthropic (X)](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2120841731302154708) · [Anthropic roadshow announcement](https://www.anthropic.com/news/roadshow-launch) · [The Neuron](https://www.theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-friday-november-13-2026/)
**Tags:** `entity:anthropic` `theme:ipo` `tier:t1` `tier:t2`

### SpaceX plans $74B+ of AI data centers; SoftBank takes $3.7B charge on power equipment
Reuters reports SpaceX plans more than $74B of AI compute campuses in Texas and Arizona; the roughly $50B Arizona site still needs county approval. Separately, Semafor reports SoftBank took a $3.7B charge after revising orders for data-center power equipment, triggering nonrefundable payments. Both are second-hand reports via The Neuron.
**Why it matters (PM):** Compute supply is being committed at huge scale while some buyers over-ordered, so don't assume either permanent scarcity or glut when planning inference costs.
**Sources:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/spacex-invest-over-74-billion-supercompute-facilities-texas-arizona-2026-11-12/) · [Semafor](https://www.semafor.com/article/11/12/2026/softbank-loses-billions-after-over-ordering-energy-equipment-for-ai-buildout/) · [The Neuron](https://www.theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-friday-november-13-2026/)

## ⚖️ Policy, Safety & Regulation

### Notion customers' AI chat transcripts exposed via Fireworks breach
TechCrunch reports attackers accessed some customer chat transcripts held by AI inference provider Fireworks. Notion says fewer than 1% of workspaces were affected.
**Why it matters (PM):** Audit which third-party model hosts retain prompts and transcripts, and make retention terms explicit in your own data-handling docs.
**Why it matters (mid-market):** Mid-market buyers ask who processes their data; a named-subprocessor breach at a market-leading app makes that question routine in security reviews.
**Sources:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/12/cyberattack-on-fireworks-ai-allowed-hackers-to-access-notion-customers-chat-transcripts/) · [The Neuron](https://www.theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-friday-november-13-2026/)
**Tags:** `entity:notion` `entity:fireworks-ai` `theme:security` `lens:mid-market` `tier:t2`

### Nonprofits sue five AI developers to halt more capable frontier models
Reuters reports the suit names OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta and SpaceX and seeks a court order halting development of models more capable than current systems. No ruling reported.
**Why it matters (PM):** Low odds of success, but litigation risk is now part of the roadmap-risk picture for any feature that depends on next-generation models.
**Sources:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/openai-google-anthropic-hit-with-lawsuit-seeking-halt-frontier-ai-development-2026-11-12/) · [The Neuron](https://www.theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-friday-november-13-2026/)
**Tags:** `entity:openai` `entity:anthropic` `entity:google` `entity:meta` `entity:spacex` `theme:litigation` `tier:t2`

### AI shopping assistants sometimes cite risky sites
A brand-protection firm's test found questionable sites in 15% of Perplexity, 8% of ChatGPT and 4% of Gemini shopping responses. Single-firm test, methodology not independently checked.
**Why it matters (PM):** If your product surfaces third-party links via an LLM, add link-reputation checks before you ship.
**Sources:** [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1032020/gemini-chatgpt-perplexity-shopping-test-risky-fake-websites-citations) · [The Neuron](https://www.theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-friday-november-13-2026/)
**Tags:** `entity:perplexity` `entity:chatgpt` `entity:gemini` `theme:safety` `tier:t2`
