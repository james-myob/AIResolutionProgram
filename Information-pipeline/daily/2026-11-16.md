# Daily AI Briefing — Mon 16 Nov 2026

## TL;DR

## 🧠 Models & Capabilities

### Edge0-145B: open-source 145B model claimed to run on a personal computer
Edge0 released Edge0-145B (and a 120B sibling), claiming over 80 tokens/second and about 29GB peak memory on compatible machines for coding and reasoning. These are vendor claims; no independent benchmarks were found.
**Why it matters (PM):** If the claims hold, on-device frontier-class models become a credible option for privacy-sensitive features, so keep a watch-list rather than a roadmap commitment until third-party evals appear.
**Sources:** [Edge0](https://edge0.ai/blog/introducing-edge0-145b-frontier-ai-capabilities-on-your-own-computer) · [The Neuron](https://www.theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-monday-november-16-2026/)
**Tags:** `entity:edge0` `theme:on-device` `tier:t2`

### Meituan LongCat-2.5: 1.6T open-weight agent model reportedly trained without Nvidia GPUs
The Decoder reports Meituan's 1.6-trillion-parameter open-weight model targets coding agents and was trained on Chinese hardware; a sample self-hosted deployment needs 194 Huawei Ascend chips.
**Why it matters (PM):** Strong open-weight agent models trained off the Nvidia stack weaken the "one supplier" assumption in compute-cost forecasts.
**Sources:** [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/meituan-releases-a-1-6-trillion-parameter-agent-model-that-never-touched-an-nvidia-gpu/) · [The Neuron](https://www.theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-monday-november-16-2026/)

## 📦 Products & Tooling

### OpenAI launches ChatGPT Wallet for agent purchases
Agents can buy on a user's behalf using a payment-network substitute for the card, so card details stay hidden from the agent, with biometric approval for each purchase. Initially for US ChatGPT Pro subscribers via dots, built with American Express, Mastercard and Visa.
**Why it matters (PM):** The consent-per-purchase pattern is a ready-made reference design for any agentic checkout or payment-approval flow you are scoping.
**Why it matters (mid-market):** Small merchants and finance products should expect agent-originated orders and payments, and decide how to authenticate and reconcile them.
**Sources:** [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-wallet/) · [The Neuron](https://www.theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-monday-november-16-2026/)
**Tags:** `entity:openai` `entity:chatgpt` `theme:agentic-commerce` `lens:mid-market` `tier:t1` `tier:t2`

### Agent access rules take shape: United blocks booking agents while Sierra's Poppy 1.0 gains adopters
United blocked unauthorised AI booking agents, framing a fight over who owns the customer relationship. Separately, Sierra's Poppy 1.0 protocol lets personal agents see which actions a business permits; Shopify enabled it across its stores, Meta's Muse uses it automatically, and 100+ companies participate.
**Why it matters (PM):** Decide now whether your product is an agent-friendly surface (publish permitted actions) or a walled one, since both postures are appearing at scale.
**Sources:** [The Neuron on United](https://www.theneuron.ai/news/united-wants-you-to-book-the-flight-your-ai-agent-isnt-invited/) · [The Next Web on Poppy](https://thenextweb.com/news/sierra-poppy-1-0-shopify-meta-muse-personal-agents)

## 💰 Business & Funding

### Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang resigns; reportedly weighing a return
Nvidia announced on 12 Nov that Huang stepped down after 33 years; CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO. Huang's exit followed press reports about an alleged relationship with an accused Chinese spy and a board-led investigation; FT reports he is considering rejoining as CEO, which Elon Musk supports. CoreWeave and Nebius sold off, and the pending $13B Hugging Face acquisition now faces questions.
**Why it matters (PM):** Nvidia roadmap and pricing signals are less predictable near term, so avoid hard-coding GPU-supply assumptions into plans.
**Sources:** [Nvidia](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-ceo-transition/) · [FT](https://www.ft.com/content/0f30a230-4d66-481b-a4fd-3a4ed0c45855) · [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/neoclouds-ai-stocks-slide-again-nvidia-turmoil-rattles-sector-2026-11-13/) · [Latent Space](https://latent.space/p/ainews-jensen-huang-resigns-after-33-years-leading-nvidia/)

## ⚖️ Policy, Safety & Regulation

### Fed survey: AI is now the top-cited financial-stability worry; US and allies agree to guard AI supply chains
In the Fed's latest survey 87% of contacts named AI-related risks, up from 50% in May, citing complex AI financing, cybersecurity and a possible earnings correction. Separately, the US and allies including Japan and India agreed to protect AI supply chains from China-linked vulnerabilities (per FT via Bloomberg), while US lawmakers push tighter chip export rules.
**Why it matters (PM):** Expect tighter scrutiny of AI spend and vendor provenance in customer procurement, so have supply-chain and cost-justification answers ready.
**Sources:** [Reuters on Fed](https://www.reuters.com/business/finance/ai-rockets-top-worries-about-financial-system-latest-fed-survey-2026-11-13/) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-15/us-allies-agree-to-guard-ai-supply-chains-from-china-ft-says) · [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1033984/congress-export-rules-ai-chips-china)
