## 💰 Business & Funding

### Anthropic IPO: valuation expectations fall as tech sells off
Per WSJ reporting relayed by AI Weekly, institutional investors told Anthropic to price its Nasdaq IPO at roughly $1.5T–$1.6T, down from about $2T, citing a tech sell-off and a wish to leave room for a first-day gain. Latent Space's 7 Nov podcast notes (citing WSJ) that some investors see the valuation nearer $1T, that run rate topped $80B in October against a $100B year-end target, and that the Nasdaq is down about 10%. AI Weekly reports the roadshow could begin as soon as this coming week with a debut targeted for the week before Thanksgiving; Anthropic's S-1 was filed 26 Oct. The share count and price range were not final.
**Why it matters (PM):** A public Anthropic will publish audited numbers and face quarterly pressure, so watch its filings for pricing and capacity signals that affect your build-vs-buy and cost models.
**Sources:** [Anthropic S-1 announcement](https://www.anthropic.com/news/s1-sec) · [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/anthropic-ipo-buyers-push-valuation-to-15t-after-tech-selloff) · [Latent Space](https://latent.space/p/ainews-anthropic-ipo-whither-now/)

## 📦 Products & Tooling

### NVIDIA targets wasted memory in AI agents
The Neuron reports NVIDIA's approach to agent memory focuses on deciding what context to keep, where to store it and when to reuse it, so long-running agents stay affordable. Only the summary was available; details are not verified here.
**Why it matters (PM):** Memory and context cost is becoming a first-order line item for agent features, so ask your platform team how much of your agent spend is repeated context.
