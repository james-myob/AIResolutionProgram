### xAI declares Grok Imagine's core build "done"
Elon Musk posted a two-word update — "Done with Grok Imagine" — on 5 July, signalling that the image/video generation feature (built on xAI's Aurora model) has moved from active development to a stable, polished state. The feature supports seven aspect ratios, multiple visual styles, and short-form video generation, with particular attention paid to accurate text rendering inside generated images.

**Why it matters (PM):** "Done" likely means feature-complete, not frozen — but it's a signal that xAI now considers multimodal generation table stakes for a frontier chat product rather than a differentiator in progress; benchmark any video-gen roadmap item against what's already shipping for free inside a general assistant.

## 🔬 Research & Papers

### Mistral's Leanstral 1.5 solves 587 of 672 PutnamBench problems
Mistral released Leanstral 1.5, a free, Apache 2.0-licensed, 6B-active-parameter model specialised in formal mathematical verification (Lean 4). It saturates the miniF2F benchmark, solves 587/672 PutnamBench problems, and posts state-of-the-art results on FATE-H (87%) and FATE-X (34%).

**Why it matters (PM):** Formal verification is a niche today, but it's the kind of capability that quietly becomes infrastructure for high-stakes code review and compliance tooling — worth a bookmark if your product touches financial, safety-critical, or regulated code paths.
**Sources:** [Mistral AI](https://mistral.ai/news/leanstral-1-5/) · [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/07/03/mistral-ai-releases-leanstral-1-5-an-apache-2-0-lean-4-code-agent-model-solving-587-of-672-putnambench-problems/)

## ⚖️ Policy, Safety & Regulation

### UN holds first-ever Global Dialogue on AI Governance in Geneva
The UN's inaugural Global Dialogue on AI Governance opened in Geneva on 6 July with delegates from 169 countries — the first time all UN member states have convened specifically on AI governance. Secretary-General António Guterres named four priorities: common safety baselines and testing methods for frontier systems, human-rights "red lines" (including an AI Child Safety Pledge), capacity-building for developing countries via a proposed Global Fund for AI, and mandatory environmental-footprint disclosure with a 2030 renewable-energy target for data centres. He also called for a ban on lethal autonomous weapons. The dialogue ran alongside the first report from the Bengio/Ressa-chaired Independent International Scientific Panel on AI, which warned that science "cannot guarantee" frontier AI won't cause catastrophic harm.

**Why it matters (PM):** A UN dialogue has no binding power, but "common baselines and shared testing methods" is the language procurement teams and regulators reach for next — if you sell into government or regulated enterprise, the safety-baseline and environmental-disclosure asks here are a preview of what RFPs will start requiring, not just diplomatic noise.
**Sources:** [UN Secretary-General's remarks](https://www.un.org/sg/en/content/sg/statements/2026-07-06/secretary-generals-remarks-the-opening-of-the-first-global-dialogue-artificial-intelligence-governance-delivered) · [UNESCO](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/un-global-dialogue-opens-urgent-call-safe-and-inclusive-ai-benefits-all) · [UN News](https://news.un.org/en/story/2026/07/1167873)

## 🛠️ Product Practice

### Anthropic: Government of Alberta uses Claude to scan 466M lines of code for vulnerabilities in 20 hours
Since 2025, Alberta's Ministry of Technology and Innovation has used Claude Code (Opus and Sonnet) to audit security across all 27 provincial ministries. The scan — 466 million lines in 20 hours, versus an estimated 6.5 years by conventional methods — fed automated remediation, including rebuilding a legacy Java subsidy portal in 4-5 days versus an original 5-month build. Alberta has also stood up "red team" / "blue team" agent reviewers checking applications against roughly 95 security controls per cycle, and published technical white papers on the approach.

**Why it matters (PM):** This is a named, government-scale reference case for "agent does the boring high-volume audit work, humans review the output" — useful ammunition if you're pitching a similar workflow internally and need a concrete before/after number rather than a vendor benchmark.
**Sources:** [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/alberta-government-claude-cybersecurity)
