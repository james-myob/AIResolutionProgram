## 🔬 Research & Papers

### OpenAI: agents complete more research tasks; models improve chip design
OpenAI reports autonomous success on research tasks that take humans two to four hours rose from 23% (March) to 46% (October), with wide variance by task. Separately, AI-generated changes to a multiplier design cut estimated chip area about 15% and a calculation from two clock cycles to one; OpenAI is working with Synopsys. Both are self-reported.
**Why it matters (PM):** Long-horizon task reliability is improving but uneven, so scope agent features to task types with proven success rates.
**Sources:** [OpenAI (research acceleration)](https://openai.com/index/research-acceleration-jagged-frontier/) · [OpenAI (chip design)](https://openai.com/index/advancing-chip-design-with-our-models/)

## 📈 Industry Analysis

### Banks put liability for AI-agent activity on customers
Citi updated its terms to hold customers responsible for activity through third-party AI tools with account access; other banks are taking similar positions.
**Why it matters (PM):** If you ship agents that act on financial accounts, expect liability, consent and audit-trail requirements to be defined by the banks' terms, not your own.
**Why it matters (mid-market):** Finance teams at mid-sized firms using agent tools on bank accounts may inherit liability they haven't priced in.
**Sources:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/18/more-banks-says-customers-using-ai-agents-are-on-their-own-even-if-they-get-hacked/)
**Tags:** `entity:citi` `theme:agents` `theme:liability` `theme:finance` `lens:mid-market` `tier:newsletter`
