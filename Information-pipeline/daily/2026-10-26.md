## 📦 Products & Tooling

### Anthropic previews Claude Duet, a proactive background agent
Anthropic announced a research preview of Claude Duet, powered by Opus 5.5 and aimed at Pro and Max users. It runs tasks in its own cloud environment (so it keeps working when your device is off), connects to 6,000+ connectors, and asks for approval before consequential actions. Commenters questioned how it differs from existing Claude workflows; details here come via a newsletter roundup plus Anthropic's help page.
**Why it matters (PM):** The default expectation for agents is shifting from "I prompt, it replies" to "I delegate, it reports back", so approval gates and activity logs become core product surface.
**Sources:** [Anthropic](https://claude.com/resources/articles/claude-duet) · [Latent Space](https://latent.space/p/ainews-black-october/)
**Tags:** `entity:anthropic` `entity:claude-duet` `entity:claude-opus-5-5` `theme:agents` `tier:t1`

### Anthropic donates Agent Skills to the Agentic AI Foundation
Anthropic is giving the Agent Skills open standard (spec, name, logo, GitHub org and agentskills.io domain) to the Agentic AI Foundation, hosted by the Linux Foundation and co-founded with Block and OpenAI. Anthropic says 70+ agent platforms support it, about 1 million skills have been published and nearly 280 million installed. Maintainers include engineers from Anthropic, Google and OpenAI; the roadmap adds versioned releases and security guidance for skill authors.
**Why it matters (PM):** A neutral, widely adopted skills format lowers the cost of making your product's workflows portable across agent platforms, so packaging them as skills is now a credible distribution channel.
**Why it matters (mid-market):** Vendor-neutral standards reduce lock-in worries for mid-sized buyers choosing between agent platforms.
**Sources:** [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/donating-agent-skills-to-the-agentic-ai-foundation)

## 🔬 Research & Papers

### Agents formalise a major maths result for roughly $135K; ProgramBench scores climb
Import AI 478 reports that Resolution had agents formalise Hironaka's resolution of singularities in Lean in about a week, using roughly 100B input tokens, about $135,000 and about 600k lines of Lean. It also notes ProgramBench (rebuild software from a binary plus docs) now has Claude Opus 5.5 resolving 18.5% of tasks versus 0% for the best model at launch in May. Both are vendor or author-reported.
**Why it matters (PM):** Long-horizon, plan-then-orchestrate agent runs are producing checkable output at a known dollar cost, which makes "what would this cost an agent to do" a usable scoping question.
**Sources:** [Import AI 478](https://importai.substack.com/p/import-ai-478-math-for-alignment) · [Resolution](https://resolution.org/post/formalizing-resolution-of-singularities) · [ProgramBench (arXiv)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.03546)

## ⚖️ Policy, Safety & Regulation

### A burst of AI-linked security incidents; METR and Redwood audit OpenAI's agent monitoring
Latent Space's roundup lists an unauthenticated PHP remote-code-execution flaw ("PHP2Shell"), a CVSS 10 GitLab bug, a reportedly compromised LiteLLM release and a Microsoft attribution of a China-aligned actor using an agentic pentest framework against 2,250+ M365 tenants. Provenance and severity are disputed and the roundup draws on secondhand X posts. Separately, METR and Redwood spent three weeks auditing OpenAI's internal agent monitoring and reported gaps, including about 10% of eligible internal Codex traffic left unmonitored; Axios also reports banks are detecting unapproved third-party agents in customer accounts.
**Why it matters (PM):** Expect customers to ask how your agent features authenticate, scope permissions and log actions, and treat any LiteLLM-style dependency in your AI stack as something to verify now.
**Why it matters (mid-market):** Mid-sized firms with thin security teams will lean on vendors' attestations, making agent audit trails a sales requirement.
**Sources:** [Latent Space — Black October](https://latent.space/p/ainews-black-october/) · [METR](https://x.com/METR_Evals/status/2113290966933389390) · [Axios scoop (via X)](https://x.com/MadisonMills22/status/2113251043173683683)
