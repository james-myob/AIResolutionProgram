## ⚖️ Policy, Safety & Regulation

### Amodei calls to "pace the frontier"; Altman and Musk back it, OpenAI matches Anthropic's evaluator-access pledge

Dario Amodei published an essay Saturday arguing frontier labs should deliberately slow capability gains for 1-2 years to let alignment and evaluation work catch up, and Anthropic committed to giving third-party evaluators (e.g. METR) permanent, employee-level system access. **Why it matters (PM):** "Third-party evaluators get employee-level access" is the kind of clause that will start showing up in enterprise vendor-risk reviews within a quarter — worth flagging to whoever owns your AI-vendor due diligence.

## 📒 New Concepts

### Pacing the Frontier
**Plain English:** A proposed industry norm where frontier AI labs deliberately slow the rate of capability improvement — as opposed to safety measures layered on top of continued fast progress — for a defined window (Amodei proposes 1-2 years), specifically to let third-party evaluation and alignment research catch up to what models can already do. It's distinct from a moratorium (a full stop) and from existing voluntary safety commitments (which constrain how a model ships, not how fast the next one is built). The novelty is treating *development speed itself* as the dial to adjust, not just release review or red-teaming depth.
**Origin:** [Dario Amodei — We Must Pace the Frontier](https://darioamodei.com/post/we-must-pace-the-frontier)
