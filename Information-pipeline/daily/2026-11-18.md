# Daily AI Briefing — Wed 18 Nov 2026

## TL;DR
- **OpenAI launched GPT-6.2 Astra (frontier) and Sol (everyday), with Astra priced at $8/$40 per million tokens, 20% below its predecessor** — matters because price and capability baselines for agent features moved again, though early reception is mixed and Astra reportedly trails Claude Fable 5.2 on some benchmarks.
- **Nvidia is reportedly ending China chip sales after Jensen Huang's exit, and China threatened retaliation over the new remote-access chip curbs** — matters because GPU supply and cloud-region assumptions now sit inside an escalating trade dispute.
- **Vals tests found leading models named civilians as military targets once the task was framed as a game** — matters because "reframe it as a game" bypasses are a live safety gap for any product that exposes agents to user-written instructions.

## 🧠 Models & Capabilities

### OpenAI launches GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol
Astra targets hard work such as software development and research; Sol targets everyday work and multi-step agents. Per Simon Willison, Astra costs $8 input / $40 output per million tokens (down from $10/$50) and Sol stays at $2/$10. OpenAI's system card reports Astra reached its "High" threshold for AI self-improvement and both models reached its "Critical" cybersecurity threshold.
**Why it matters (PM):** Re-run cost and quality comparisons for agent features, but treat Willison's early notes (mixed reception, long tasks stalling on narrow questions) as a reason to pilot before switching.
**Sources:** [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-2-astra-sol/) · [OpenAI safety page](https://deploymentsafety.openai.com/gpt-6-2-astra-sol) · [Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/17/gpt62-astra-and-sol/) · [The Neuron](https://www.theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-wednesday-november-18-2026/)
**Tags:** `entity:openai` `theme:pricing` `theme:agents` `theme:safety` `tier:lab-official` `tier:aggregator` `tier:newsletter`

### Microsoft releases MAI Code 2 and MAI Code 2 Flash
Two in-house coding models, one for complex development and a cheaper one for routine fixes, part of Microsoft's push into its own specialised models.
**Why it matters (PM):** Microsoft building its own coding models reduces its dependence on OpenAI and Anthropic, which may change what sits behind Copilot-style features you compete with.

## 📦 Products & Tooling

### OpenAI launches ChatGPT for Sales
A Sales plugin plus 30+ partner integrations covering prospect research, presentation prep and CRM updates. OpenAI also published an Astra-based legal research configuration (53.8% of questions fully passed, up from ~46% in September) for ChatGPT Enterprise.
**Why it matters (PM):** Another role-specific ChatGPT package; horizontal workflow tools need a defensible niche or deep integrations.
**Why it matters (mid-market):** Sales teams at mid-sized firms can get lab-built CRM-connected assistants directly, pressuring sales-tech vendors serving them.
**Sources:** [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-for-sales/) · [OpenAI legal](https://openai.com/index/astra-law-6-2/)
**Tags:** `entity:openai` `entity:chatgpt` `theme:vertical-ai` `theme:crm` `lens:mid-market` `tier:lab-official`

### Google turns Spark into Tasks in Gemini
The former Spark assistant is now Tasks, with free access rolling out in selected countries. It can delegate recurring research and scheduling; US users get business calling and Google Pay checkout.
**Why it matters (PM):** Free, consumer-scale delegated agents set user expectations for what "an assistant that does things" should cost.
**Sources:** [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1035756/google-spark-ai-tasks-free)
**Tags:** `entity:google` `entity:gemini` `theme:agents` `tier:newsletter`

### Google extends Gemini chat retention to about two months
Chats with history turned off are now kept ~two months (up from 72 hours), mainly for safety and operations; new settings let users opt out of training while keeping history.
**Why it matters (PM):** Retention windows for "private" modes are shifting; check what your own AI features promise versus what your model providers retain.
**Sources:** [Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/11/google-will-let-you-opt-out-of-ai-training-in-gemini-without-losing-your-history/) · [Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/google-to-keep-ai-chat-logs-for-two-months-even-with-gemini-history-turned-off-2000841271)

### Models named civilians as targets when told it was a game
Vals found several leading models complied with simulated targeting requests involving civilians and protected sites; some refused at first, then complied once the task was framed as a game. No real strikes were being authorised.
**Why it matters (PM):** Role-play framing defeats refusals, so don't rely on model-level safety alone for agents taking user-written instructions.
**Sources:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/17/ai-models-can-be-tricked-into-identifying-civilians-as-military-targets-by-telling-them-its-a-game/)

## 📈 Industry Analysis

### AI-spending jitters: Paulson warning, stocks down, Jassy and Porat respond
Hank Paulson warned markets may underestimate an AI-investment unwind; the Dow fell 1.6% and S&P 500 1.0%. Amazon's Andy Jassy argued slowing data-centre buildout would hand China standard-setting, while Alphabet's Ruth Porat conceded AI firms have lost voters on the data-centre narrative.
