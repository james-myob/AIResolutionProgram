## 📦 Products & Tooling

### Claude Code's "auto mode" becomes the default for Pro, Max, and Team plans
Starting today, new Claude Code sessions on Pro, Max, and Team plans default to auto mode: instead of asking approval for every command, a classifier screens each action and blocks anything irreversible, destructive, or reaching outside the working environment. Anthropic's own testing found the classifier caught a planted dangerous command 89% of the time versus 13.6% for human reviewers approving line-by-line, and the classifier's token overhead is now free on those plans.

**Why it matters (PM):** Re-test your team's Claude Code workflows against this new baseline before assuming last week's approval habits and audit trails still apply — the default just flipped from human-gates-every-action to automated-guardrails-plus-spot-check.
**Why it matters (mid-market):** Teams without a dedicated security reviewer get a stronger automated default for free — but check whether your compliance posture still needs manual approval logs, since auto mode changes what gets recorded.
**Sources:** [Anthropic](https://claude.com/blog/auto-mode-default-in-claude-code) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/09/anthropic-is-turning-claude-codes-auto-mode-on-by-default/) · [Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Aug/8/auto-mode/)

## ⚖️ Policy, Safety & Regulation

### Anthropic confirms Claude now watermarks generated text and files worldwide
Anthropic confirmed that Claude models released after August 2, 2026 automatically embed invisible watermarks in generated text and files, fulfilling its commitment under the EU AI Act's Article 50(2) Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-Generated Content. The watermarking applies globally, not only to EU users; existing models have until December 2 to be upgraded to support it.

**Why it matters (PM):** If your product embeds Claude output into customer-facing documents, that content is now silently watermarked — audit any workflow where invisible markup (or its removal) could matter for provenance, plagiarism-detection, or "human-authored" contractual claims.
**Sources:** [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-text-watermark) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/11/anthropic-says-it-will-watermark-text-generated-by-its-ai-models/) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/12/anthropic-claude-watermarks-ai-detection)
