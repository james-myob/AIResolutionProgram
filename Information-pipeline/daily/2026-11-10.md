## 🧠 Models & Capabilities

### Fable 5.2 tops Artificial Analysis, but cost per task rose
Latent Space's roundup says Artificial Analysis ranks Fable 5.2 #1 on its Intelligence Index v5 (score 65). SemiAnalysis reports its GDPx cost per task rose 31% ($67.16 to $88.15) versus Fable 5.1, because the model runs more tests and subagents even though the token price is 20% lower. Several evals show the max and xhigh effort settings scoring below high, and Anthropic suggests starting at medium or high. Safety classifiers require data retention by default; zero data retention is limited to eligible enterprise customers. These figures are relayed from posts on X, not checked at the original posts. Follows yesterday's launch.
**Why it matters (PM):** Re-run your unit-economics sheet on cost per completed task, not per token, and default to high effort rather than max.
**Why it matters (mid-market):** Customers with zero-data-retention contract terms may not be able to use Fable 5.2 unless they qualify as eligible enterprise accounts.
**Sources:** [Latent Space](https://latent.space/p/ainews-fable-5-2-retakes-world-1-llm-spot-as-ant-ipo-roadshow-begins/) · [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-2)

### Google opens Gemini 4 Carbon to everyone
Gemini 4 Carbon, first limited to a subset of cyber defenders about two weeks ago, is now generally available at $2/$10 per million input/output tokens. Per Simon Willison, that price rises on 1 December to $4/$20, matching Claude Opus 5.5. It offers three thinking levels (default medium) and works in Google's Antigravity IDE, including a /swarm feature for coordinating subagents.
**Why it matters (PM):** The December price step means any bake-off against Fable 5.2 is cheapest to run in the next three weeks.
**Sources:** [Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/10/llm-gemini/)

### Meta launches Muse Ember, a very cheap small model
Meta's Muse Ember 1.0 is priced at $0.10/$0.50 per million tokens for prompts up to 200k tokens, aimed at agent subtasks such as routing, extraction and long-document parsing. Artificial Analysis puts it at about 34 on its Intelligence Index for roughly $0.12 per task, level with Claude Haiku 5.5 (about 34.5 at $0.25), and Meta claims nothing scores higher for less. Latent Space reports it is available on Google Cloud.
**Why it matters (PM):** Cheap routing and extraction tiers are now a price-competitive option from a third vendor, which strengthens your hand when renegotiating or multi-sourcing the low end.
**Why it matters (mid-market):** Sub-$1 output pricing makes always-on AI features viable on mid-market per-seat price points.

## 🛠️ Product Practice

### Anthropic: tell Fable 5.2 the codebase is "yours" to get refactoring
Anthropic's prompting guide says that without ownership framing the model tends to add to existing structure even when that repeats code. Telling it "This codebase is yours: what it's for and how it's built are your call" made it merge repeated code in about half of attempts versus under a tenth, in Anthropic's small tests.
**Why it matters (PM):** Whether your agent refactors or just piles on is a prompt-level choice, so decide it deliberately in your coding-agent templates.
**Sources:** [Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/10/prompting-claude-fable-52/)
