## 💰 Business & Funding

### Apple sues OpenAI for trade secret theft; the lawsuit reignites the Musk-Altman feud
Apple filed suit against OpenAI in federal court in Northern California on 10 July, alleging that OpenAI's hardware chief Tang Tan (a former Apple VP) and ex-Apple engineer Chang Liu took confidential hardware designs, supplier data, and device prototypes to build OpenAI's consumer hardware business — the same business OpenAI entered by acquiring Jony Ive's io Products for $6.4B. Apple's complaint says the extraction happened "at every level" of its former hardware and on-device AI teams. The story spilled into a public weekend spat: Elon Musk called Altman a "scam," Altman fired back about Musk's SpaceX datacentre timelines, and both used the moment to plug their own recent frontier-model launches.

**Why it matters (PM):** This is a preview of the IP fights every incumbent hardware or platform company will have as AI labs recruit their device and on-device-AI talent — worth tightening IP-assignment and non-solicitation language on any hire from a company you also partner with.

## 📦 Products & Tooling

### Google, Microsoft, Salesforce, Snowflake, and ServiceNow back a rival standard to Anthropic's MCP
The five companies agreed to support a shared technical standard for connecting AI agents to enterprise data and tools, according to The Information — a direct response to Anthropic's Model Context Protocol, which became the de facto cross-vendor standard after Anthropic donated it to the Linux Foundation's Agentic AI Foundation in December 2025. The fight is over the plumbing layer that decides how agents reach a company's CRM, data warehouse, and workflow systems, not model capability.

**Why it matters (PM):** Nothing breaks today if you're building on MCP, but a second vendor-blessed standard from the companies that run your customers' core systems means budgeting engineering time for protocol interoperability rather than assuming one winner.
**Why it matters (mid-market):** Salesforce, Snowflake, and ServiceNow are exactly the platforms most mid-market software sits on top of — if this alliance ships a rival spec, smaller ISVs building agent integrations will likely need to support both protocols, and integration cost lands hardest on vendors who can least absorb it.
**Sources:** [The Information](https://www.theinformation.com/newsletters/applied-ai/google-microsoft-team-beat-back-anthropic-openai) · [unrot.co roundup](https://unrot.co/blogs/today-top-10-ai-news-july-13-2026)

## ⚖️ Policy, Safety & Regulation

### EU AI Act's chatbot-disclosure and deepfake-labelling rules become enforceable 2 August — fines retroactive to 2025
The EU AI Act's Article 50 transparency obligations — chatbot disclosure, synthetic-content marking, deepfake labelling — become enforceable across the EU's single market on 2 August, and the European Commission gains fining power over general-purpose AI providers, applicable retroactively to violations dating back to August 2025. As of mid-2026, only around ten of the EU's 27 member states have visible enforcement infrastructure in place, and roughly two dozen labs — including Anthropic, Google, Microsoft, and Mistral — have signed the voluntary GPAI Code of Practice.

**Why it matters (PM):** If your product embeds a GPAI model and serves EU users, confirm chatbot-disclosure and content-labelling is live before 2 August — the grace period is ending and enforcement is retroactive.
**Why it matters (mid-market):** Code of Practice signatories are almost entirely the model labs, not the app layer built on top — mid-market SaaS companies embedding AI need their own Article 50 compliance plan rather than assuming the model provider's sign-off covers them.
**Sources:** [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/320101/20260710/eu-ai-act-enforcement-here-chatbot-rules-live-high-risk-ai-delay-now-binding-law.htm) · [Pearl Cohen](https://www.pearlcohen.com/new-guidance-under-the-eu-ai-act-ahead-of-its-next-enforcement-date/) · [asanify](https://asanify.com/blog/news/eu-ai-act-enforcement-july-13-2026/)
