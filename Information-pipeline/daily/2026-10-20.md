## 🧠 Models & Capabilities

### DeepSeek ships V4.1-Flash-1017 (MIT) as US procurement restrictions tighten
DeepSeek released V4.1-Flash-1017, a 552B-parameter mixture-of-experts model (16B active per token, which keeps serving cheaper) with image input, under the MIT licence, and published its DeepInfer inference stack. Interconnects also reports a US DHS order barring DeepSeek from federal civilian procurement and a proposed (not enacted) Chinese rule requiring government pre-approval before frontier models are shared internationally, with automatic approvals reportedly planned for open-source technology.
**Why it matters (PM):** Licence and benchmark are only two of the filters now; where a model comes from can rule it out for customers who sell to US government.
**Why it matters (mid-market):** Mid-sized vendors with public-sector customers should ask early whether those contracts restrict model provenance before embedding a Chinese open-weights model.

## 📦 Products & Tooling

### Hugging Face adds native TPU support via TorchTPU
Transformers, Diffusers and Kernels now run on TPU through TorchTPU, a PyTorch backend that treats TPU as another device (`device_map="tpu"`). Hugging Face reports 96.5% of the TPU-run Transformers tests pass, and a Hub kernel speeds up decoding 21.5× on Qwen3-30B-A3B at batch size 64 (vendor-reported).
**Why it matters (PM):** Another non-NVIDIA path to serve open models lowers switching cost between hardware, which strengthens your position when negotiating inference capacity.
**Sources:** [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/blog/hf-torchtpu)

## 🔬 Research & Papers

### IBM ALTK-Evolve: learned guidelines beat fine-tuning on τ²-bench customer-service tasks
The method distils guidelines from an agent's past attempts at training tasks and appends them to the system prompt, with no weight updates. On τ²-bench retail, a GPT-4.1 agent's Pass^4 (success on all four of four tries) rises from 57.5% to 67.5%, above the fine-tuned RAFT-30B-A3B at 61.4%; on airline, a GPT-5.5 agent reaches 82.0%, top of the leaderboard. These are IBM's own results.
**Why it matters (PM):** If reliability gains come from a prompt-level memory you own, agent quality becomes a product-data problem rather than a model-vendor problem.
**Why it matters (mid-market):** Customer-service agents are a common mid-market use case, and this approach needs no fine-tuning budget or labelled data.
**Sources:** [Hugging Face / IBM Research](https://huggingface.co/blog/ibm-research/altk-evolve-bc)

## ⚖️ Policy, Safety & Regulation

### Ai2 FairStress: models drift toward minority-coded candidates under adversarial prompts
Ai2's benchmark has 300 two-candidate decision scenarios across 29 domains, expanding to over 13 million test cases, plus a 50K-item core subset. Under matched adversarial prefixes, frontier models picked minority-coded candidates 8.8 to 42.4 percentage points more often than majority-coded ones. Counterfactual chain-of-thought most reduced the gap while largely preserving accuracy.
**Why it matters (PM):** If your product uses an LLM to rank, screen or approve people, fairness testing now has a public benchmark you can run before a customer or regulator asks.
**Sources:** [Hugging Face / Ai2](https://huggingface.co/blog/allenai/fairstress)
