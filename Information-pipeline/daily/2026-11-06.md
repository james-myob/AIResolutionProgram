## 🧠 Models & Capabilities

### Thinking Machines, Baidu and Google ship new models
Thinking Machines released Inkling 2 Small (594B total parameters, about 17B active per request) aimed at coding, vision and autonomous work; it is available via Tinker, with weights to follow after more safety testing. Baidu released ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base (280B, about 14B active) as an open-source base model before instruction tuning. Google's Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash makes video clips up to 15 seconds and cut video pricing 30% to $0.07 per second at 720p. Benchmark claims are self-reported.
**Why it matters (PM):** Another day of cheaper, more specialised models; keep your model choice behind an abstraction so you can swap on price and task fit.
**Sources:** [Thinking Machines](https://thinkingmachines.ai/news/inkling-2-small/) · [Latent Space](https://latent.space/p/ainews-inkling-2-small-17a-594b-trained-in-68-hours-on-12t-tokens-beating-muse-spark/) · [The Decoder (ERNIE)](https://the-decoder.com/baidu-releases-ernie-5-5-flash-base-with-280-billion-parameters-as-an-open-source-foundation-model/) · [Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/introducing-gemini-omni-1-2-flash/)

## 📦 Products & Tooling

### Perplexity, Mistral and Cursor all ship unattended agents
Perplexity launched "Teammates", always-on work agents that monitor changes and use existing project permissions (Max subscribers, web). Mistral added Apps and Managed Agents to Studio in public preview, with private cloud and on-premises deployment planned for late November. Cursor's cloud agent can now build an app, set up a database and auth, and deploy it to a shareable link, with security checks first and user approval required on paid plans.
**Why it matters (PM):** Inspectability and approval gates are shipping as headline features, which tells you buyers will ask for them in your agent features too.
**Why it matters (mid-market):** Mistral's on-prem and private cloud option and Perplexity/Cursor's approval steps address the data-control and oversight objections mid-sized buyers raise first.
**Sources:** [The Verge (Perplexity)](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1028302/perplexity-teammates-ai-agents-work) · [Mistral on X](https://x.com/MistralAI/status/2118370229466153299) · [Cursor on X](https://x.com/cursor_ai/status/2118458629091426304) · [The Neuron](https://www.theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-friday-november-6-2026/)
**Tags:** `entity:perplexity` `entity:mistral` `entity:cursor` `theme:agents` `theme:agent-governance` `lens:mid-market` `tier:t2`

### Claude can read your bank accounts (beta)
Anthropic's beta lets U.S. Pro, Max and Ultra subscribers link accounts through Plaid and ask Claude about spending, bills and unusual transactions. Access is read-only, covers up to two years of history, and Personal Finance conversations are excluded from training.
**Why it matters (PM):** A frontier lab is moving into regulated personal-finance data via an aggregator; note the read-only and no-training defaults as a template for sensitive-data features.
**Why it matters (mid-market):** Finance and accounting software vendors now have a general-purpose assistant competing for the "ask questions about my money" interface.
**Sources:** [Claude on X](https://x.com/claudeai/status/2118403114890137895) · [The Neuron](https://www.theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-friday-november-6-2026/)

## 🔬 Research & Papers

### METR: Anthropic's capability progress about 2x faster, evidence largely redacted
METR's 5 November assessment estimates Anthropic's rate of AI capability progress is roughly double its earlier pace, partly because AI tools speed up internal research. Anthropic withheld most of the evidence METR found most informative, citing confidentiality, so the headline figure is hard to verify externally.
**Why it matters (PM):** If labs' own AI tools are compressing release cycles, plan roadmaps assuming model capability steps arrive faster and less predictably.
**Sources:** [METR](https://metr.org/blog/2026-11-05-anthropic-ai-rd-acceleration-assessment/) · [METR on X](https://x.com/METR_Evals/status/2118412183860351084) · [The Neuron](https://www.theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-friday-november-6-2026/)

## ⚖️ Policy, Safety & Regulation

### OpenAI launches GPT-6 Cyber with restricted access
OpenAI's GPT-6 Cyber helps authorised researchers find vulnerabilities and build exploits to test defences. OpenAI rates its cyber capability "Critical" and limited initial access to selected organisations. It follows yesterday's company-reported Linux exploit pipeline.
**Why it matters (PM):** Expect capability-tiered, vetted-access launches for dual-use features; build identity and eligibility checks into any product that wraps such models.
**Sources:** [OpenAI deployment safety](https://deploymentsafety.openai.com/gpt-6-cyber) · [The Neuron](https://www.theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-friday-november-6-2026/)
**Tags:** `entity:openai` `theme:security` `theme:gated-release` `tier:t1`

### US Justice Department forms an AI enforcement task force
The department created a task force targeting illegal uses of advanced AI, especially by foreign governments and criminal groups, plus AI-related fraud, and wants companies to keep misuse safeguards in place.
**Why it matters (PM):** Misuse safeguards are becoming an enforcement expectation, not just a policy nicety; document yours.
**Sources:** [DOJ on X](https://x.com/TheJusticeDept/status/2118387344944308552)
**Tags:** `entity:doj` `theme:ai-policy` `theme:enforcement` `tier:t5`

## 📈 Industry Analysis

### AI-infrastructure doubts: stocks slip, data-centre limits spread, Google commits $17B in Wyoming
Investors are questioning whether the AI build-out keeps its pace, and local governments from New York to Amsterdam are tightening data-centre rules. Google still announced a $17B Wyoming data centre partly powered by a planned 702 MW fuel-cell project. OpenAI separately pledged more transparency with local officials and to end mandatory NDAs.
**Why it matters (PM):** Power and permitting are becoming constraints on compute supply; that is a risk to price drops you may be assuming.
**Sources:** [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/05/cnbcs-experts-on-whether-the-ai-trade-is-ending-and-what-investors-should-do-next.html) · [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/where-authorities-are-restricting-data-centres-amid-ai-boom-2026-11-05/) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-05/google-plans-to-spend-17-billion-on-wyoming-data-center) · [The Neuron](https://www.theneuron.ai/news/openai-data-center-reset-no-ndas-public-officials/)
**Tags:** `entity:google` `entity:openai` `theme:data-centers` `theme:ai-cost` `tier:t5`

### Airbnb plans an AI travel agent for H1 2027; USA Today blames AI search for traffic decline
Airbnb says its agent will combine maps, photos and comparisons rather than text chat alone, and will help change bookings and fix problems mid-stay. Separately, USA Today's CEO cited changing search habits as a factor in weaker traffic and digital revenue after poor Q3 results.
**Why it matters (PM):** One incumbent is building the agent in-house while another loses distribution to AI search; both argue for owning your customer interface and being discoverable.
**Why it matters (mid-market):** Vertical marketplaces are building their own agents, so mid-market SaaS in travel and hospitality should expect them as both competitors and integration targets.
**Sources:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/05/airbnb-says-its-launching-an-ai-travel-agent-in-the-first-half-of-2027/) · [Poynter](https://www.poynter.org/business-work/2026/usa-today-co-ai-use-advertising-video/)
