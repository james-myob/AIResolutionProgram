## 🧠 Models & Capabilities

### Google unveils Gemini 4 Carbon, limited to cyber defenders for now
Google's post calls Carbon its strongest model for cybersecurity and long-horizon agentic coding, building on Gemini 4 Argon. It reports 88.2% on SEC-bench Pro (finding vulnerabilities), 54.8% on ExploitGym and 71.0% on CWE-bench (patching), and says Terminal-Bench 4.0 cheating fell to 0.0%. Access starts with a subset of cyber defenders via DeepMind's Fairwind Program; developers, businesses and consumers get it "soon". Latent Space, relaying Artificial Analysis (secondhand), says Carbon is #1 on the Intelligence Index at 59 against Opus 5.5's 58, but scores 59 on the Coding Agent Index in Antigravity CLI versus 62 for Claude Code with Opus 5.5, and its hallucination rate rose from 4% to 11%.
**Why it matters (PM):** Capability rankings are tight at the top and the lead is one point on a third-party index, so the useful signal is the cyber-first, gated release pattern rather than the leaderboard.
**Why it matters (mid-market):** Security tooling vendors and buyers should expect frontier vulnerability-finding capability to reach defenders first, with general availability and price still unsettled.
**Sources:** [Google blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-carbon/) · [Latent Space (secondhand evals)](https://latent.space/p/ainews-gemini-4-carbon/)

## 📦 Products & Tooling

### GitHub Copilot Fusion mixes Anthropic and OpenAI models at runtime
Per Latent Space's roundup, GitHub's Copilot Fusion orchestrates Anthropic and OpenAI models during a task, and GitHub claims up to 77% lower cost. Independent results from Artificial Analysis are also relayed secondhand and I did not verify either against GitHub's own page.
**Why it matters (PM):** If runtime routing across labs is bundled into the IDE, the model choice moves from your product decision to the platform's.
**Sources:** [GitHub's Kyle Daigle on X](https://x.com/kdaigle/status/2115490644143874425) · [Latent Space (secondhand)](https://latent.space/p/ainews-gemini-4-carbon/)

### OpenAI adds monthly API credits for ChatGPT Pro and Business
ChatGPT Pro subscribers can claim $100–$500 a month in API credits by tier, and Business plans pool up to $500 per workspace, according to OpenAI Developers.
**Why it matters (PM):** Bundling API credit into subscriptions lowers the barrier for individual builders to prototype on OpenAI models, which can shape which APIs early products are built on.

Analyst Jamin Ball says the roughly $80B first-half 2026 net loss in Anthropic's S-1 is mostly a mark-to-market increase in convertible-note and SAFE liabilities rather than operating burn. This is an analyst's reading relayed by Latent Space; I did not check it against the filing.
