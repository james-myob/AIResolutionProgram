## 📦 Products & Tooling

### Anthropic replaces git and its review tooling as agent-driven merges grow 12x
Anthropic says nearly all clients of its monorepo are always-online agents and CI jobs, which clashes with git's offline-client design. Merges rose from about 2,500 per day in May 2026 to over 30,000 per day by September, and main-branch history alone is nearly 100 GB. Its in-house Scion moves commits, merges and history queries to the server and serves sparse checkouts through tiered caches; Anthropic reports infrastructure about 200 times smaller than the old git mirrors and CI checkouts in seconds rather than minutes (vendor-reported).
**Why it matters (PM):** If your teams are adopting coding agents, plan for review, CI and repository capacity to become the bottleneck before model quality does.
**Sources:** [Anthropic Engineering](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/redesigning-source-control) · [Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/)
**Tags:** `entity:anthropic` `theme:agentic-coding` `theme:developer-infrastructure` `tier:t1`

### DeepMind and Singapore's GovTech build an on-device scam detector on Gemma
The two fine-tuned a lightweight Gemma model on local scam conversations to run entirely on the device. A lightweight classifier screens incoming messages, and suspicious conversations go to the fine-tuned model for deeper analysis. Google cites Singapore data: investment fraud, fake job offers and official impersonation make up over half of reported crimes, with more than S$4 billion lost since 2019.
**Why it matters (PM):** A small open model fine-tuned locally and run on-device is a working pattern for privacy-sensitive features where sending data to a cloud API is a non-starter.
**Sources:** [Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/on-device-ai-scam-detection-mddi/)

## ⚖️ Policy, Safety & Regulation

### Anthropic sets out its 2026 US midterms safeguards
Claude.ai will show official Democracy Works voting information for US users, including state-specific polling and ID answers and election-night results. Anthropic reports 99% on its political evenhandedness evaluation for the 5.5 models, though independent reviewers found unsolicited caveats and uneven hedging. External red-teams (ISD, McCain Institute) found Claude refused single harmful requests but could miss campaigns split into harmless-looking tasks, prompting new multi-step misuse classifiers; about 0.51% of a 250,000-conversation US sample concerned elections.
**Why it matters (PM):** Test any agent feature against decomposed requests, not just single bad prompts, since per-message safeguards are the weaker layer.
**Sources:** [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/2026-us-midterms)
