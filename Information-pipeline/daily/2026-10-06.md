## 🧠 Models & Capabilities

### Mistral launches Large 4: 1T-parameter mixture-of-experts, open weights promised by end of October
Mistral Large 4 has 1 trillion total parameters but activates only 49 billion per request (a mixture-of-experts design, which keeps running costs down). It is natively multimodal, covers 160+ languages, and is in public preview via Mistral Studio at $1.36 per million input tokens and $4.18 per million output tokens; Mistral says weights will be released by the end of October. Simon Willison notes it scores 38 on Artificial Analysis, a big jump from Large 3's 9 but behind DeepSeek 4.1 Flash, and calls it about six months behind the frontier. Benchmark figures in Mistral's post are vendor-reported.
**Why it matters (PM):** A cheap, multimodal, soon-to-be self-hostable model widens your options for cost-sensitive features, but test it on your own tasks since independent rankings put it below the leaders.
**Why it matters (mid-market):** Mistral pitches EU-hosted and on-premise deployment, which speaks directly to mid-market customers in regulated or data-residency-constrained markets.

## ⚖️ Policy, Safety & Regulation

### Anthropic expands its Cyber Verification Program into three access tiers
Anthropic combined Project Glasswing and the original Cyber Verification Program into one program with Defense, Red Team and Specialized tiers. Defense covers incident response, malware analysis and vulnerability work (reviewed within days); Red Team adds authorised penetration testing (reviewed over weeks, individuals ineligible); Specialized is for organisations testing critical safety systems and is reviewed with the US government. Real-time blocks remain on actions that could cause physical harm or mass disruption, such as ransomware deployment.
**Why it matters (PM):** Tiered, identity-verified access to riskier model capabilities is becoming the standard pattern, so plan a verification path rather than a binary on/off if your product exposes sensitive capabilities.
**Sources:** [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/cyber-verification-program)
