## 🛠️ Product Practice

### "We're going to need default hard budget caps on pretty much everything"
Willison says soft billing alerts are not enough now that coding agents can spin up pay-per-use services autonomously, leaving developers exposed to surprise bills overnight. He points to AWS's spending limits (launched September 2026), which pause a project when usage hits its limit, and Google Cloud's Spend Caps (July) as the right direction, and wants hard caps on by default with an opt-out checkbox.
**Why it matters (PM):** If your product bills by usage or calls metered AI APIs, design a hard cap (not just an alert) into the default plan before agentic usage makes runaway cost a support issue.
**Why it matters (mid-market):** Mid-market buyers rarely have a FinOps team to catch runaway bills, so default spend limits are a selling point and a churn-prevention feature.
