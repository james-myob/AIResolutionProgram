## ⚖️ Policy, Safety & Regulation

### Anthropic widens independent evaluator access, with METR getting "employee-like" access
Starting 15 Oct, six METR employees get company hardware, office space and internal tools, and METR will publish its risk assessments. Anthropic cannot veto publication but can require redaction of predefined categories such as customer data and model architecture details. SecureBio is reviewing the chemical and biological weapons chapter of Anthropic's August 2026 Risk Report, Accenture and Faculty are doing red-teaming and safeguard testing, and talks with Hugging Face and others are early.
**Why it matters (PM):** "Can the evaluator publish without veto?" is now a concrete question to put in vendor risk reviews.
**Sources:** [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/an-update-on-independent-evaluators)
**Tags:** `entity:anthropic` `entity:metr` `theme:safety` `theme:eval` `tier:t1`

### Anthropic publishes midterm-election safeguards and usage data
In a 21-27 Sep sample of 250,000 US Claude.ai conversations, about 5.75% touched politics, 0.51% elections or voting, and roughly 0.002% violated election-integrity policy. Claude will pull state-specific voting details from Democracy Works, and Anthropic added classifiers after outside researchers found it could miss campaigns split into individually harmless requests; it also banned accounts tied to a fake news site. The 99% evenhandedness score is Anthropic's own evaluation.
**Why it matters (PM):** If your product lets users generate persuasive or civic content, the split-into-harmless-requests gap is a misuse pattern to test for in your own guardrails.
**Sources:** [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/2026-us-midterms)
**Tags:** `entity:anthropic` `theme:safety` `theme:elections` `tier:t1`

## 🛠️ Product Practice

### How the Claude Code team does code review
Addy Osmani, Morgan Westlee Lunt and Qing Wang argue "did a human read the code?" is an imprecise proxy; the better question is which check, automated or human, should catch a given risk. Their review runs continuously, "more like spellcheck while you write", so Claude reviews and fixes most changes before a human sees them, leaving reviewers the higher-level questions.
**Why it matters (PM):** List your top delivery risks and assign each to an automated or human check, rather than defaulting to "a human reviews everything".
**Sources:** [Claude Code blog](https://claude.dev/blog/solving-code-review-at-anthropic/) · [Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Oct/16/code-review/)
