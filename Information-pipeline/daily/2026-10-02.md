## 📦 Products & Tooling

### Anthropic merges Cowork and Chat into one experience
Users can now ask a question or hand off a longer task that keeps running after the laptop is closed. Projects, artifacts, connectors and skills carry across both modes. Reported by The Neuron; I did not reach Anthropic's own announcement.
**Why it matters (PM):** Delegate-and-leave is becoming the default interaction model, so scope features around long-running, resumable tasks rather than single-turn chat.
**Sources:** [The Neuron](https://theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-thursday-october-1-2026/)
**Tags:** `entity:anthropic` `theme:agents`

### Anthropic launches $100M Claude Frontier Academy
Anthropic will train 10,000 Frontier Deployed Engineers by end of 2027. The programme is modelled on medical training: a multi-day bootcamp, a graded practical assessment, a 12-week residency leading Claude projects at the engineer's own organisation, then a credential. Initial cohorts run in San Francisco, New York and London with engineers from Accenture, Bain, Capgemini, Commonwealth Bank of Australia, Deloitte, McKinsey, Morgan Stanley and Novo Nordisk; first credential holders are expected in early 2027.
**Why it matters (PM):** Anthropic is building a certified implementation channel, so expect integrators to arrive at your shared customers with Claude-credentialed staff.
**Why it matters (mid-market):** The first cohorts are large consultancies and enterprises, so credentialed talent will be scarce and expensive for mid-market firms; packaged, low-deployment-effort AI features gain an edge.
**Sources:** [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-frontier-academy)
**Tags:** `entity:anthropic` `theme:enterprise-adoption` `theme:talent` `lens:mid-market`

## ⚖️ Policy, Safety & Regulation

### OpenAI dismisses three safety researchers and warns 100+ organisations over agent activity
The WSJ reportedly says the researchers shared infrastructure architecture with an outside AI-safety group. Separately, Reuters reports OpenAI warned more than 100 organisations about unauthorised activity tied to its agents, and California's Attorney General served an investigative subpoena over cybersecurity incidents. This follows the FTC probe covered yesterday. All via The Neuron.
**Why it matters (PM):** Agent incidents are now producing notifications and subpoenas, so confirm you can show customers what your agents accessed and when.
**Sources:** [The Neuron](https://theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-thursday-october-1-2026/)
**Tags:** `entity:openai` `entity:ftc` `theme:safety` `theme:regulation` `theme:agents`

### Hawley and Murphy propose AI Agent Accountability Act
The bipartisan bill would create civil and criminal liability for certain hacking by AI agents, depending on knowledge, recklessness and safeguards in place. Transluce separately documented agent traffic against U.S. and Canadian government sites, including 200,000+ requests to the Education Department's civil-rights site.
**Why it matters (PM):** "Safeguards in place" is a liability factor, so documented rate limits and permission controls are a legal as well as a quality feature.
**Sources:** [The Neuron](https://theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-thursday-october-1-2026/)
