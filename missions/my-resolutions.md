## Resolution 4: Build a More AI-Driven Culture in My Team
**Goal:** Do a quick 5-10 minute demo at an existing team meeting after each mission — show what I built, what worked, what didn't, and spark discussion.

**Format:** Quick demo slot at a regular team meeting. Low overhead — no separate calendar invite, no slide deck required. Just screen-share what I made and talk through it.

**Why this works:** It's sustainable (no extra meetings), visible (people see real output), and creates a natural prompt for others to share what they're building too.
