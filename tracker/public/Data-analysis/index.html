Three insights

1Mid-market insolvency has tripled in five years — and it's structural, not COVID-rebound

FY24–25 insolvencies are 2.5–6× the FY20–21 base across nearly every industry: Professional Services +565%, Health Care +357%, Manufacturing +284%, Construction +148%. The FY20–21 baseline was suppressed by JobKeeper and safe-harbour provisions, but the post-2021 curve is steeply upward. Cash-flow management is now the price of entry for any AU mid-market ERP value layer.

2Health Care & Social Assistance is the standout invest opportunity — and Acumatica has packaged it globally; MYOB hasn't packaged it for AU

Fastest-growing mid-market segment (+6.5% YoY), second-largest by count (7,784 businesses with 20+ FTE), moderate risk (4.5/1,000). NDIS providers, private aged care, allied health groups, primary care networks. Acumatica's healthcare modules exist; the AU-specific layer (NDIS chart-of-accounts, HICAPS/Medicare billing, SCHADS award compliance, multi-site reporting) is the unbuilt opportunity.

3The turnover lens reveals 3× more mid-market opportunity than headcount alone

Real Estate has 1,952 mid-market businesses by employment but 17,460 by turnover (8.9×); Financial Services 6.9×; Construction 6.1×; Agriculture 6.0×. These are small-team, high-revenue businesses (property syndicates, advisory firms, specialist subbies, large family farms) — genuinely mid-market commercially but invisible to a headcount-based segmentation. Likely 40–60k AU businesses sitting between MYOB Business and MYOB Mid-Market today.

Three actions

A. Make cash-flow control the #1 horizontal investment for the next 12 months

Accelerate AR automation, cash-flow forecasting, credit-risk flagging. Target the four highest-volume, highest-rate mid-market segments: Construction (69 insolv, 10.6/1k), Admin & Support (58, 10.0/1k), Other Services (33, 21.7/1k), Transport (21, 8.4/1k). Together they account for 181 of 393 mid-market insolvencies (46%). Brand-fit is exact — MYOB's strength is financial control.

B. Stand up an AU Health Care & Social Assistance vertical pack

Use Acumatica's existing healthcare modules where possible; build the AU-specific layer (NDIS, HICAPS, SCHADS, multi-entity). Goal: capture meaningful share of the +6.5% mid-market growth in 18 months. The only top-5 strategic-rank segment growing materially — every other large segment is flat or declining.

C. Re-segment GTM around turnover bands as well as headcount, prioritising Real Estate, Financial Services, and Construction subbies

Run a Q3 discovery sprint with channel partners to identify the small-team, high-revenue businesses currently in the gap between MYOB Business and MYOB Mid-Market. Capturing 10% of the estimated 40–60k addressable population would meaningfully grow the customer base. Counter-call: deprioritise hospitality vertical investment — Accommodation & Food Services is largest but contracting fastest (–5.1%) with low risk. That's consolidation, not failure. Serve, don't over-invest.
